## How to Use This Knowledge Base

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for Your Topic">
    Use the search bar at the top of the page to find articles by keyword, service name, tool, or process. Most answers are a single search away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse by Service Area or Section">
    Use the left-hand sidebar to navigate by department or service. Each section is organised to match the way our teams are structured, so you can drill down from a high-level overview to detailed SOPs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flag Missing Content to Ops">
    If you can't find what you're looking for, don't assume it doesn't exist — flag the gap to the Operations team so we can prioritise creating or migrating that content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suggest Updates via the Team Channel">
    Spotted something outdated or incorrect? Post a note in the designated knowledge base update channel so the responsible team lead can review and action it promptly. Keeping this resource accurate is a shared responsibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This knowledge base is strictly **internal and confidential**. All content is intended solely for Hellenic Technologies staff. Do not share links, screenshots, or content from this portal with clients, vendors, or external parties without explicit approval from your team lead or the Operations department.
</Note>
