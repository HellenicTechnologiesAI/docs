hellenicAI is the AI division of Hellenic Technologies, accessible at [app.hellenicai.com](https://app.hellenicai.com). The platform automates and augments agency services through a suite of AI-powered tools — with AI Assistants, OmniChannel Chatbot (OmniAI), and B2B Outreach currently live and available to clients, while Google Ads AI, Meta Ads AI, SEO AI, Social Media AI, Websites AI, Analytics AI, and Designs AI are actively in development or planned for future release. This page gives agency staff a full picture of the platform's service lineup, pricing model, and the steps required to onboard a client.

## Pricing Model

hellenicAI is priced at **€49 per seat per month**, plus AI model usage billed by consumption (i.e., tokens or API calls made to underlying models). There are no setup fees and no long-term contracts. Every new service added to the platform is automatically included in the existing subscription — clients do not need to upgrade or re-contract to access new capabilities as they launch.

When quoting clients, make sure to explain that the seat fee covers platform access and infrastructure, while model usage is variable and depends on volume. For high-volume deployments, prepare a usage estimate alongside the seat cost.

## Onboarding a Client to hellenicAI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account at app.hellenicai.com">
    Register the client's account on the hellenicAI dashboard. Use the client's business email as the primary account holder. You can add additional seats for team members or agency staff during or after setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the relevant service(s)">
    From the dashboard, choose which hellenicAI service(s) the client is activating — AI Assistants, OmniChannel Chatbot, B2B Outreach, or any combination. Each service is provisioned independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dedicated AI server provisions automatically">
    Once a service is activated, a dedicated AI server is provisioned for the client in under 5 minutes. Each client gets an isolated environment — their data is never shared with or accessible by other clients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and go live">
    Complete the service-specific configuration (knowledge base, personas, channels, sequences, etc.) and run pre-launch testing. Refer to the individual service guides for detailed configuration steps. Once testing is complete, hand over access to the client or go live on their behalf.
  </Step>
</Steps>
