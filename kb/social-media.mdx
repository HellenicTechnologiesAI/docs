The Social Media team manages organic presence across platforms — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok — for clients on the **Social as you GO: Knight** retainer. This guide covers the end-to-end monthly workflow, platform-specific considerations, approval processes, and reporting standards that every Social Media Manager and Account Manager should follow.

## Content Planning

Every client's organic social activity is governed by a **monthly content calendar**, planned and produced in advance. The calendar must be reviewed and approved by the client at least **5 business days before the first scheduled publication date** of the month. This allows time for revision rounds without disrupting the publishing schedule.

The content calendar documents:
- Post date and time (per platform)
- Platform and format (e.g., Instagram Reel, LinkedIn text post, Facebook image post)
- Copy (caption text, hashtags)
- Visual asset reference (linked from the creative folder)
- Status (Draft / In Review / Approved / Scheduled)

Store all content calendars in the client's folder on the shared drive under `/Clients/[ClientName]/Social Media/Content Calendars/`.

## Monthly Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Strategy Brief With Client or Account Manager">
    At the start of each month (or during the final week of the prior month), align on the content themes, key dates, campaigns, and objectives for the coming month. This can be a short call with the client or a briefing from the Account Manager. Document the agreed themes in the content calendar before moving to ideation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Content Ideation and Creation">
    Develop the content plan: post topics, copy drafts, and a creative brief for any visual assets. Submit the creative brief to the **Creative Design team** at least **7 business days before the calendar approval deadline** to allow time for design, revision, and final delivery. Write captions in the client's brand voice — refer to the brand guidelines in the client folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client Review and Approval">
    Share the completed content calendar (with copy and visuals attached) through the **approval workflow tool** (see your Account Manager for the tool used per client). Do not use email chains for approval — all feedback must be logged in the tool for audit purposes. Chase for approval proactively; if no response is received within 3 business days, escalate to the Account Manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule Posts Across Platforms">
    Once approved, schedule all posts using the agency's social media scheduling tool. Confirm scheduling is correct: right platform, right account, right date and time, correct visual asset attached. For time-sensitive posts (e.g., breaking news, same-day campaigns), coordinate directly with the Account Manager for a fast-track approval process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Community Management">
    Monitor all client social accounts daily during business hours. Respond to comments, DMs, and mentions within **24 hours**. For negative comments or complaints, use the client's escalation protocol (documented in their client folder). Never delete negative comments without client approval. Flag anything sensitive to the Account Manager immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monthly Performance Report">
    At the end of each month, pull performance data from each platform's native analytics. Compile the monthly report using the Social Media Monthly Report template (shared drive: `/Templates/Reporting/Social Media/Monthly Report.pptx`). Include: reach, impressions, engagement rate, follower growth, top-performing posts, and a written commentary with recommendations for next month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Platform-Specific Notes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Instagram & TikTok">
    **Reels and short-form video consistently outperform static image posts** on both platforms in reach and engagement. Always brief the Creative Design team on video content early — video production takes longer than static assets.

    - Aim for at least 2 Reels per month per client on Instagram
    - TikTok content should feel native and authentic — overly produced content underperforms
    - Use trending audio where brand-appropriate (confirm music licensing with the creative team)
    - Optimal posting times: check each client's Instagram Insights for their specific audience activity windows
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="LinkedIn">
    LinkedIn is the primary platform for **B2B clients**. Content tone should be professional, insightful, and value-driven.

    - Thought leadership posts (industry commentary, data insights) and team culture posts perform well
    - Avoid overly promotional content — LinkedIn audiences disengage quickly from hard-sell copy
    - Articles and document posts (carousels in PDF format) drive strong engagement for B2B audiences
    - Tag relevant company pages and individuals when appropriate (with client approval)
    - Optimal posting frequency: 3–4 times per week; quality over quantity
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Facebook">
    While organic reach on Facebook has declined significantly, the platform remains **strategically important for retargeting audiences** — organic post engagement builds the Custom Audiences used by the Meta Ads team.

    - Coordinate with the Paid Media team to ensure high-performing organic posts are boosted when relevant
    - Facebook Groups can be valuable for community-focused clients — discuss with the Account Manager if applicable
    - Video and link posts perform better than image posts in the current algorithm
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  **Batch your content creation** for maximum efficiency. Rather than producing content week by week, set aside one focused session per client each month to create 2–4 weeks of content in one go. Brief the creative team for the full month's assets in one request. This approach reduces back-and-forth, gives the design team better visibility into workload, and means the calendar is ready for client approval well ahead of the deadline.
</Tip>
