hellenicBI is the data and business intelligence practice at Hellenic Technologies, delivering pipelines, warehouses, dashboards, and marketing attribution solutions for enterprise and mid-market clients. This page covers the full service catalog, typical tech stack, and guidance on how to scope and sell BI engagements.

## Typical Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Data Warehouse | BigQuery (default), Redshift, or Snowflake |
| Transformations | dbt (data build tool) |
| Data Ingestion | Fivetran, Stitch, or custom-built pipelines |
| Visualization | Looker Studio, Power BI |
| Orchestration | Cloud Composer (Airflow) or equivalent |

The stack above is the HT default. Deviations should be documented in the project plan and agreed with the delivery lead.

## Working with BI Clients

<Tip>
  Always start new BI engagements with a **BI Consulting** engagement before recommending a tech stack or scoping a build. Clients often underestimate the complexity of their data landscape. A readiness assessment uncovers the real scope, protects the delivery team from mid-project surprises, and positions HT as a strategic partner rather than just a vendor.
</Tip>

### Scoping Guidance

- **Small clients / early data maturity**: BigQuery + Looker Studio is usually sufficient. Keep it simple.
- **Mid-market with existing tools**: Meet the client where they are. Integrate with their existing warehouse or CRM exports before recommending a platform migration.
- **Enterprise**: Full stack — warehouse, dbt transformations, Fivetran ingestion, Power BI for enterprise BI teams who prefer it.

### Marketing Attribution Engagements

Marketing attribution projects require close collaboration between the hellenicBI team and the Digital Marketing team. Ensure both teams are looped in during scoping. You'll typically need:

- Ad platform data (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn — via API or Fivetran connector)
- CRM data (HubSpot, Salesforce, or equivalent)
- GA4 / web analytics event data (via BigQuery export)
- A defined attribution window and model agreed with the client

<Note>
  **Managed BI is always the goal.** After every BI delivery, pitch the Managed BI retainer. Position it as ensuring the client's data infrastructure doesn't drift or break as their business evolves. Long-term BI relationships are among the stickiest retainers HT offers — the client becomes dependent on the dashboards and pipelines we build. See the [After Sales](/kb/after-sales) page for retainer packaging guidance.
</Note>
