## Reporting Cycle

All digital marketing campaigns produce a **monthly performance report** as standard, delivered to the client by the account manager. For active performance campaigns — particularly those with aggressive KPIs or significant spend — **weekly check-in reports** are issued instead, summarising key metrics, spend pacing, and any optimisations made that week.

Report templates for each channel live in the shared drive under `/Templates/Reporting/Digital Marketing/`.

<Tip>
  Always align creative assets with campaign objectives **before** launch — not after. Brief the Creative Design team as early as possible in the planning phase. Mismatched creative (e.g., brand-awareness visuals used on a direct-response conversion campaign) is one of the leading causes of underperformance at launch. Use the creative brief template in the shared drive to document objectives, formats, dimensions, and messaging direction.
</Tip>
