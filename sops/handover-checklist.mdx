A proper handover ensures clients have everything they need to operate their new service or website, and sets up the after-sales relationship correctly. Follow this checklist for every project completion — a thorough handover is the final impression you leave, and it directly influences client satisfaction, retention, and referrals.

<Note>
  The Account Manager owns the handover process end-to-end. The PM is responsible for ensuring all delivery-side items are complete. Both must sign off before the project is marked closed.
</Note>

## Handover Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the Documentation Package">
    Compile and send the client a complete documentation package before or during the handover call. The package should include all materials relevant to the service delivered:

    - **User guides** — step-by-step instructions for managing their CMS, dashboard, ad accounts, or tools
    - **Brand guidelines** (if a branding or design project was in scope)
    - **Analytics and tracking documentation** — what is tracked, how, and where to find it
    - **Credentials and access list** — a clear record of all platform logins, admin accounts, and API keys created during the project
    - **Platform login details** — ensure all credentials are recorded and transferred securely (use the approved password manager or secure document method, never plain-text email)

    Use the standard HT document templates from the shared drive. Name all files using the convention: `[ClientName]_[ServiceType]_[YYYY-MM]`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run the Handover Training Session">
    Schedule and deliver a dedicated training call with the client's relevant team members. Use screen-share to walk through:

    - CMS administration (for website projects)
    - Dashboard navigation and reporting (for analytics or BI projects)
    - Ad account structure and basic management (for digital marketing projects)
    - Any other platform or tool the client will be managing independently

    **Record the session** (with the client's permission) and share the recording in the client folder and with the client. Training recordings are referenced repeatedly by client teams after handover and reduce repeat support queries significantly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Transfer All Access">
    Confirm the client has full admin-level access to every platform, account, and tool that belongs to them. Then:

    - Remove any temporary Hellenic Technologies team accounts from client platforms
    - Transfer ownership of any accounts created under HT credentials to the client's own accounts
    - Confirm the client can log in to every platform independently before the call ends
    - Document any access that will be retained by HT for after-sales management (with client consent)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Obtain Final Sign-Off">
    Get written client approval confirming they accept the delivery. An email sign-off is acceptable. The sign-off should confirm:

    - The delivered work meets the agreed scope
    - The client has received and reviewed all documentation
    - Any outstanding minor items (snag list) are agreed and scheduled

    This sign-off is your formal record of project completion. Store it in the client folder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Present After-Sales Options">
    The AM presents relevant managed service and retainer options from the Hellenic Technologies after-sales portfolio. This is a natural conversation, not a hard sell — the client has just completed a successful project and you are offering continuity:

    - **Managed servers / website maintenance**
    - **Managed digital marketing** (ongoing campaign management)
    - **Managed web analytics**
    - **Managed BI**
    - **Managed design** retainer
    - **Retainer hours** or **SLA/BAU retainer** for ongoing support

    Log any interest or decisions in the CRM.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Internal Handover to After Sales">
    Brief the After Sales team in writing before closing the project internally. The briefing must include:

    - Client background and key contacts
    - Services delivered and any ongoing managed services agreed
    - Known preferences, sensitivities, or recurring issues
    - Any open items or commitments made during the project
    - Links to the client folder and relevant project documentation

    Do not assume the After Sales team has access to the project history — give them everything they need to serve the client from day one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Close the Project in the PM Tool">
    Mark the project as complete in the project management tool. Archive all project files in the client folder following the HT naming and folder structure convention. Confirm:

    - All tasks are closed or transferred
    - No open items remain unassigned
    - Final invoice has been issued by Finance (if applicable)
    - Project folder is tidy and navigable for future reference
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request NPS Score or Testimonial">
    Send a satisfaction survey or a Trustpilot review request using the **standard HT email template** (available in the shared drive). Time this request immediately after a successful handover — client satisfaction is at its peak right after a smooth project completion.

    <Tip>
      The best time to ask for a Trustpilot review or testimonial is **within 48 hours of handover**, while the client is still in a positive, project-complete mindset. Waiting too long means the moment passes. A genuine, timely review request converts at a significantly higher rate than a follow-up weeks later.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Documentation Standards

All documents produced for client handover must conform to the following standards.

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Templates** | Use the approved HT document templates stored in the shared drive. Do not create freeform documents. |
| **File naming** | `[ClientName]_[ServiceType]_[YYYY-MM]` — e.g., `AcmeCorp_GoogleAds_2025-06` |
| **Storage location** | All files stored in the client folder in the shared drive. No files on personal drives or local machines only. |
| **Version control** | Append `_v2`, `_v3` etc. for revised documents. Never overwrite a final version — keep the history. |
| **Sensitive data** | Credentials and access details must be shared via the approved secure method. Never send passwords in plain-text email. |
