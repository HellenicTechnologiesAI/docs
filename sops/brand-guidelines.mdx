## Logo Usage

Always treat the logo with care. Misuse of the logo — even minor distortions — undermines brand credibility with clients.

- **Always** use the official logo files from the brand assets folder in the shared drive. Do not recreate or export the logo yourself.
- **Minimum clear space**: leave clear space around the logo equivalent to the height of the logo "H" on all four sides.
- **Never** stretch, rotate, skew, recolor, add drop shadows, outlines, gradients, or any other effects to the logo.
- Use the **dark logo** on light backgrounds and the **light logo** on dark backgrounds. Do not place any logo version on a busy or low-contrast background.
- Do not place the logo inside shapes, boxes, or badges unless using an approved lockup from the brand assets folder.

## Brand Colors

Use only the approved palette below. Do not substitute with approximations or off-palette colors.

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Orange | `#ef7025` |
| Light / Accent | Peach | `#eec9bc` |
| Dark / Teal | Teal | `#4fa1ab` |
| White | White | `#ffffff` |
| Dark Text | Near Black | `#1a1a1a` |

When in doubt, lead with orange (`#ef7025`) as the primary brand color. Teal (`#4fa1ab`) works well as a secondary accent on digital assets. Avoid using peach (`#eec9bc`) as the dominant color — it is a supporting accent only.

## Typography

Refer to the shared drive brand assets folder for the approved font files and usage guide. Use only approved fonts in client-facing materials. Do not substitute with system defaults like Arial or Times New Roman in formal documents unless a client is working within a constrained environment.

General guidance:
- Use **display/heading weights** for titles and section headers.
- Use **regular weight** for body copy.
- Maintain consistent type hierarchy across a document — do not mix font sizes arbitrarily.
- Left-align body text as a default. Centred text is acceptable for short headings only.

## Tone of Voice

The way we write and speak represents the agency. Follow these principles in all client-facing content — proposals, reports, emails, presentations, and social media.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use Second Person" icon="user">
    Address the reader directly as "you." This keeps copy focused on the client's needs, not on us.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lead With Outcomes" icon="chart-line">
    Open with results and business impact, not a list of features or activities. Clients care about what they gain, not what we do.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Be Short and Direct" icon="align-left">
    Keep sentences short. Remove filler words. If a sentence takes more than two reads to understand, rewrite it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Match the Audience" icon="users">
    Avoid jargon unless you are writing for a technical audience. If you must use technical terms, define them briefly on first use.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Do not** use casual language, slang, or humour in formal client communications unless you have an established personal relationship and the tone is clearly appropriate.
