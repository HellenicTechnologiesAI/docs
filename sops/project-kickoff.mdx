The project kickoff meeting marks the official start of every client engagement at Hellenic Technologies. A well-run kickoff aligns the team and client on scope, timelines, responsibilities, and communication norms — reducing rework and misalignment down the line. Use this SOP for every new project, regardless of size or service type.

<Tip>
  **Record your kickoff calls** (with the client's explicit permission). The recording becomes an invaluable reference point throughout the project — especially when scope or requirements are disputed later. Store the recording in the client folder immediately after the call.
</Tip>

## Pre-Kickoff Checklist

Complete every item on this checklist **before** the kickoff meeting is scheduled. If any item is outstanding, do not proceed to kickoff.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm Contract & Payment">
    Verify that the client contract is fully signed and that the deposit or first invoice payment has been received and cleared by Finance. Do not proceed with any kickoff preparation until this is confirmed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Client Folder">
    Set up the client folder in the shared drive, following the standard HT folder structure and naming convention. All project files, assets, briefs, and deliverables live here from day one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign the Full Project Team">
    Confirm all roles are filled: Project Manager, relevant specialists (e.g., Paid Media, SEO, Developer, Designer), and the Account Manager. Every team member must be briefed before the kickoff call takes place.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Draft the Project Plan">
    The PM creates an initial project plan in the project management tool, including high-level milestones, delivery phases, and dependencies. This draft is shared with the client during the kickoff — it does not need to be final, but it must be substantive.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Discovery Notes as a Team">
    The entire assigned team must read the discovery call notes and the signed proposal before the kickoff. No one should walk into the kickoff meeting cold. Use the internal briefing time to align on approach and flag any open questions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request Platform Accesses">
    Send the client an access request list for any platforms, accounts, or tools needed (e.g., Google Ads, GA4, CMS, social accounts). Include this in the pre-kickoff communication so access can be granted before or immediately after the call.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send Kickoff Agenda to Client">
    Send the kickoff meeting agenda to the client at least **24 hours in advance**. This allows them to prepare, invite additional stakeholders, and arrive ready to make decisions — not just listen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Kickoff Meeting Agenda

A standard kickoff runs **60 minutes**. Stick to the agenda — if discussion overruns, park items in the open-items list for follow-up rather than extending indefinitely.

| Time | Agenda Item |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | **Introductions** — team intros from both sides, roles and responsibilities |
| 5–15 min | **Project overview & scope confirmation** — walk through what was agreed, confirm nothing has changed |
| 15–25 min | **Goals and success metrics** — align on KPIs, what good looks like, how progress will be reported |
| 25–35 min | **Timeline and milestones** — walk through the project plan, confirm key dates, flag dependencies |
| 35–45 min | **Communication plan** — agree on tools (email, Slack, PM tool), meeting cadence, who the client's single point of contact is, and expected response times |
| 45–60 min | **Q&A and open items** — capture all open items with named owners and due dates |

<Note>
  The PM is responsible for running the meeting to time. The AM manages the client relationship dynamics in the room. Specialists should contribute during their relevant sections and hold questions until the Q&A block unless they are blocking a decision.
</Note>

## Post-Kickoff Actions

Complete all of the following within **24–48 hours** of the kickoff call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send Meeting Notes">
    The PM sends a written summary of the kickoff meeting to the client within **24 hours**. The summary must include: decisions made, scope confirmed, timeline agreed, communication plan, and a full list of open items with owners and deadlines.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Resolve Open Items">
    Assign every open item captured during the meeting to a named owner (internal or client-side) with a clear due date. Track these in the project management tool — do not let open items sit in an email chain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update the Project Plan">
    Incorporate any changes, clarifications, or new information from the kickoff into the formal project plan. Republish the updated plan in the PM tool and notify the team.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch Internal Team Sprint">
    The PM kicks off the first internal sprint or work phase immediately after post-kickoff admin is complete. Assign tasks, set internal deadlines, and confirm each team member knows their first deliverable and due date.
  </Step>
</Steps>
