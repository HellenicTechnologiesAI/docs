Hellenic Technologies is ISO/IEC 27001 and 27701 certified and operates with GDPR compliance built into every service we deliver. As a team member, you are responsible for understanding and following these guidelines in your daily work. GDPR compliance is not just a legal obligation — it is a core part of our value proposition to clients, especially those in regulated industries.

---

## Key GDPR Principles

All personal data processing at Hellenic Technologies must comply with the following principles. If you are ever unsure whether an activity is compliant, stop and check with the DPO before proceeding.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lawful Basis" icon="scale-balanced">
    Only process personal data when you have a valid legal basis: consent, legitimate interest, performance of a contract, or a legal obligation. Document which basis applies before you start.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Minimisation" icon="filter">
    Only collect and process the data you actually need for the task at hand. Do not gather extra fields "just in case" — this creates unnecessary compliance risk.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Purpose Limitation" icon="bullseye">
    Use data only for the purpose it was collected for. If you want to use it for a different purpose, you need a new legal basis or fresh consent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Storage Limitation" icon="trash-clock">
    Do not retain personal data longer than necessary. Follow the retention schedules defined in the Data Retention Policy in the shared drive.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Security" icon="shield-halved">
    Protect all personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures. See the Security Practices guide for detailed controls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accountability" icon="clipboard-check">
    Be able to demonstrate compliance. Document your data flows, legal bases, and processing activities. If it's not documented, it didn't happen in the eyes of a regulator.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Service-Specific GDPR Rules

Different service lines have specific compliance requirements. Review the tab relevant to your team.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Analytics & Tracking">
    **Always implement consent mode.** No tracking pixels, scripts, or tags may fire before the user has given consent through the cookie banner.

    - Implement Google Consent Mode v2 on all new and active tracking implementations.
    - Document all data flows in a Data Flow Register before going live. This includes what data is collected, where it goes, how long it is retained, and what legal basis applies.
    - Server-side tracking setups must be hosted within the EU and documented as part of the client's privacy notice.
    - Never implement tracking on a client site without a live, compliant cookie banner and privacy policy in place first.
    - Retain implementation documentation in the project folder — auditors will ask for it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="AI Services">
    **Client data in hellenicAI runs on isolated EU servers.** You must never move client data outside the agreed environment without written authorisation.

    - All hellenicAI instances are deployed in dedicated, isolated environments. Data from one client never comingles with another.
    - Client data is **never** used for model training, fine-tuning, or any AI improvement purposes. This is a contractual and operational commitment.
    - When onboarding a new client to hellenicAI, confirm the data residency region is documented in the client record before deployment.
    - Any request to integrate third-party AI tools into a client's hellenicAI environment must go through a DPIA (Data Protection Impact Assessment) before implementation.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Digital Marketing">
    **Lawful basis must be documented before any data processing for marketing purposes begins.**

    - **B2B outreach**: legitimate interest is the most common basis for B2B email outreach. Before any campaign, document the legitimate interest assessment (LIA). A template is available in the shared drive.
    - **Email marketing**: consumer (B2C) email marketing requires opt-in consent. Store proof of consent (timestamp, source, consent wording) and make it retrievable.
    - **Retargeting and audience data**: ensure all pixel and audience data collection has a valid consent mechanism on the source site before building audiences.
    - **Meta and Google ad accounts**: use only approved data sharing configurations. Do not enable data sharing settings beyond what the client has consented to.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web Development">
    **Every new website we build must launch with a compliant cookie banner and privacy policy in place.**

    - Do not launch a website without a working GDPR cookie consent banner connected to a proper consent management platform (CMP).
    - The privacy policy must accurately reflect the data collected on the site. Coordinate with the client's legal team or use the approved template — do not write a privacy policy from scratch.
    - Cookie audits must be conducted before launch and documented. All cookies must be categorised correctly (necessary, functional, analytics, marketing).
    - For websites with user accounts or checkout flows, ensure there is a documented process for handling data subject rights requests (access, deletion, portability).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Data Subject Rights Requests

A data subject right is a request from an individual about their personal data — for example, a request to access, delete, or export data we hold about them. These requests carry legal deadlines (typically 30 days under GDPR).

**If a client's customer or any individual contacts Hellenic Technologies about their data rights:**

1. Do **not** attempt to respond directly to the individual.
2. Escalate immediately to the **DPO / Legal team** via the escalation channel in the team directory.
3. Log the request with the date and time received — the 30-day clock starts on receipt.
4. Confirm to the individual that their request has been received and is being processed (a simple acknowledgement only — no commitments about outcome).

<Warning>
If you suspect a data breach or any security incident involving personal data — including accidental disclosure, unauthorised access, or loss of a device containing client data — you must report it to the DPO within **1 hour** of becoming aware. Do not investigate or attempt to remediate on your own first. GDPR requires us to notify the relevant supervisory authority within **72 hours** of becoming aware of a qualifying breach, and that clock starts from the moment the breach is first discovered internally.
</Warning>

<Note>
All hellenicAI instances run on dedicated, isolated EU servers. Client data never leaves the client's own environment, is never shared between clients, and is never used to train or improve AI models. This is a firm operational and contractual commitment — if a client asks, you can confirm this with confidence.
</Note>
