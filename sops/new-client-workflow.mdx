This SOP defines the complete workflow for bringing a new client from initial contact through to active account status at Hellenic Technologies. Every team member involved in sales, account management, and delivery should follow this process to ensure a consistent, professional experience from the very first touchpoint.

<Warning>
  **Never start work without a signed contract and cleared payment.** Do not assign resources, create assets, or begin any delivery until the client has returned a signed contract **and** the deposit or first invoice has been paid. No exceptions — regardless of verbal agreement, urgency, or relationship.
</Warning>

## End-to-End Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lead Capture">
    The lead enters the pipeline via the website contact form, a referral, or direct outreach. Log it in the CRM immediately — every lead must have a record before any follow-up action is taken. Assign an Account Manager and set the opportunity stage to **Lead**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initial Contact">
    The assigned Account Manager reaches out within **1 business day** of the lead being logged. Use this first contact to qualify the opportunity using the **BANT** framework:

    - **B**udget — does the prospect have a realistic budget for the service?
    - **A**uthority — are you speaking to a decision-maker, or do you need to reach one?
    - **N**eed — is there a genuine, defined problem or goal we can address?
    - **T**imeline — when do they need to start or see results?

    Update the CRM with qualification notes and advance the stage to **Qualified** if BANT criteria are met.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Discovery Call">
    Schedule a **30–60 minute discovery call** to go deeper. Your goal is to fully understand:

    - The client's business, industry, and target audience
    - Their current marketing/tech stack and pain points
    - Key competitors and market positioning
    - Goals, KPIs, and what success looks like to them
    - Budget range and decision-making process

    Log detailed notes in the CRM and the client folder. These notes feed directly into the proposal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Proposal">
    The Account Manager coordinates with the relevant team leads (e.g., Web Dev, Digital Marketing, Analytics) to build a tailored proposal. The proposal must be sent within **3–5 business days** of the discovery call.

    Include:
    - Scope of work and deliverables
    - Timeline and key milestones
    - Pricing and payment terms
    - Relevant case studies or credentials

    <Tip>
      Use case studies from [hellenictechnologies.com/case-studies](https://hellenictechnologies.com/case-studies) to back up your proposal. Matching a case study from the client's industry or a comparable use case significantly increases conversion rates.
    </Tip>

    Update CRM stage to **Proposal Sent**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Proposal Presentation">
    Walk the client through the proposal on a dedicated call — do not just email it and wait. Address questions in real time, clarify scope, and handle objections. If negotiation is required, loop in the relevant team lead or department head before agreeing to changes in scope or pricing. Update the CRM to **Negotiation** if the proposal is under revision.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contract & Invoice">
    Once the proposal is agreed:

    1. **Legal** sends the client contract for signature
    2. **Finance** sends the first invoice (deposit or full first payment per agreed terms)
    3. Obtain the signed contract before any work is scheduled
    4. Confirm payment clearance before kicking off delivery

    Update CRM to **Closed Won** upon receipt of signed contract. If the deal falls through at any stage, mark as **Closed Lost** and log the reason.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Client Onboarding">
    Initiate the full onboarding process as defined in the **Client Onboarding Process** in the Knowledge Base. This includes creating the client folder, setting up the PM tool, gathering all required assets and accesses, and assigning the delivery team.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kickoff Meeting">
    Run a formal kickoff call with the client and the full project team. Follow the **Project Kickoff SOP** for preparation, agenda, and post-kickoff actions. This is the moment the client relationship formally transitions from sales to delivery.
  </Step>

  <Step title="First Milestone Delivery">
    Deliver the first agreed output **on time and to QA standards**. First impressions in delivery set the tone for the entire relationship. Communicate proactively if anything is at risk. After delivery, confirm the client has reviewed and accepted the output, and set clear expectations for the ongoing delivery cadence.
  </Step>

  <Step title="30-Day Account Health Check">
    At the 30-day mark, the Account Manager conducts a structured check-in with the client:

    - Review satisfaction with delivery and communication
    - Identify any unresolved issues or scope gaps
    - Flag any risks or upsell opportunities
    - Log findings in the CRM and escalate any red flags to the department head

    This checkpoint is the first formal signal of account health and should not be skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## CRM Requirements

Logging discipline in the CRM is non-negotiable. Every client interaction — calls, emails, proposals, negotiations — must be recorded.

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | Contact entered, not yet qualified |
| **Qualified** | BANT criteria assessed, opportunity confirmed viable |
| **Proposal Sent** | Proposal delivered to client |
| **Negotiation** | Proposal under revision or commercial discussion ongoing |
| **Closed Won** | Contract signed, payment initiated |
| **Closed Lost** | Deal did not proceed — reason must be logged |

Keeping the CRM accurate allows the entire team — from sales to finance to delivery — to have a single source of truth for every client relationship.
