B2B AI Outreach is a live hellenicAI service that automates lead generation and outbound sales outreach, using AI to personalise messages at scale across a defined target audience. It is available through the hellenicAI platform and is designed for clients who want to run structured, data-driven outbound campaigns without the manual overhead of traditional prospecting. This guide covers how to plan, configure, launch, and monitor a B2B Outreach campaign from start to finish.

## Use Cases

B2B AI Outreach is suited to a range of outbound scenarios. When scoping a campaign with a client, identify which of the following use cases applies — this shapes how the sequences and targeting are configured:

- **Lead qualification** — engage a prospect list and identify who is in-market
- **Cold outreach** — first-touch outreach to a defined ICP audience with no prior relationship
- **Meeting scheduling** — campaigns with a single CTA: book a call or demo
- **Follow-up sequences** — re-engage leads who did not respond to prior outreach or previous campaign touchpoints

## Launching a B2B Outreach Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) with the client">
    Before building any list or writing any messages, align with the client on who they are trying to reach. Document the ICP in detail: industry, company size, geography, job titles, seniority levels, and any exclusions. A well-defined ICP is the foundation of a high-performing campaign — vague targeting leads to poor reply rates and wasted send volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build or upload the target list">
    Construct the prospect list using LinkedIn (Sales Navigator export or manual research), third-party databases, or a client-supplied CSV. Validate the list for data quality — check for missing fields, duplicate entries, and contacts that fall outside the agreed ICP. Upload the finalised list to the hellenicAI platform in the required format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the messaging sequences">
    Write the full outreach sequence for the campaign. A standard sequence includes:
    - **Intro message** — first touch, short and personalised, clear value proposition
    - **Follow-up 1** — sent if no reply, adds a new angle or piece of value
    - **Follow-up 2** — sent if still no reply, lighter touch, easy to respond to
    - **Close** — final message, acknowledges no response, leaves the door open

    AI drafts an initial version of each message using the ICP and campaign brief — a strategist reviews and refines all copy before the campaign is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure AI personalisation settings">
    Set the personalisation variables the AI will use to customise each message — company name, contact name, industry, recent news or triggers (if available), and any other fields present in the target list. Higher personalisation typically improves reply rates but requires richer data in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the send schedule and daily limits">
    Configure when messages are sent (days of week, time of day in the contact's timezone where possible) and the maximum number of messages sent per day. Respect daily limits to protect sender domain reputation — do not override limits without consulting the AI team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve all messages before launch">
    Every message in every sequence must be reviewed and approved by the account strategist and, where required, the client before the campaign goes live. Check for tone, accuracy, compliance, and personalisation quality. No campaign launches without sign-off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor replies and hand off warm leads">
    Once the campaign is live, monitor the reply inbox daily. Categorise replies (positive, negative, out-of-office, not the right contact) and hand warm leads — those expressing interest or requesting a meeting — to the client promptly. Timely follow-up on warm leads is critical to campaign ROI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Metrics to Track

Review these metrics regularly during and after the campaign. Use them in client reporting and to inform optimisation decisions.

| Metric | Benchmark Guidance |
|---|---|
| Open rate | Indicates subject line and sender reputation health |
| Reply rate | Primary measure of message relevance and list quality |
| Positive reply rate | Warm leads as a percentage of total replies |
| Meetings booked | Ultimate conversion metric for most campaigns |

There are no universal benchmarks that apply to every client or industry — establish baseline expectations with the client before launch and compare against those, rather than generic industry averages.

<Note>
  **GDPR compliance is mandatory for all B2B Outreach campaigns.** Outreach must only be sent to B2B contacts where there is a valid legitimate interest basis under GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Do not use personal consumer data or rely on assumed consent. Before any campaign launches, confirm with the client that the contact list has been compiled lawfully, document the legitimate interest basis, and ensure the outreach messaging includes an easy opt-out mechanism. If you are unsure whether a list or campaign approach is compliant, escalate to the legal/compliance team before proceeding.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many contacts can we include per campaign?">
    The number of contacts per campaign depends on the client's goals, their domain sending infrastructure, and the agreed scope. Typical campaigns run between **100 and 500 contacts per sequence**. Larger volumes are possible but require additional sender domain warm-up and capacity planning — coordinate with the AI team before committing to a list size above 500. Starting with a smaller pilot cohort is often the right approach for a first campaign with a new client.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who writes the outreach messages?">
    The AI platform generates initial drafts of all messages in the sequence based on the ICP brief and campaign objective. An account strategist then reviews, edits, and finalises the copy before it goes to the client for approval. The strategist is responsible for the quality and compliance of the final messages — AI drafts are a starting point, not a finished product.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How are replies handled once the campaign is live?">
    The reply inbox is monitored daily by the assigned team member (typically the account manager or a dedicated outreach specialist). Each reply is categorised and actioned: warm leads (positive replies, meeting requests) are handed off to the client immediately; negative replies and unsubscribe requests are processed and the contacts removed from active sequences; out-of-office replies may trigger an automatic follow-up once the contact returns. Set clear SLAs with the client for how quickly warm leads will be passed over.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
