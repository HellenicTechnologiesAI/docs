ChatGPT Ads has no native GA4 linking. The site already sends the same canonical events to GA4 through `HT \| GA4 *` and `HT \| SST GA4`. That is the reason to keep GA4: first-party reporting plus the GA4 client that carries `event_id` into sGTM for Meta / LinkedIn / ChatGPT CAPI. Do not add a ChatGPT-specific GA4 event name. Do not mark `purchase` as a GA4 key event.
