After-sales services are a core revenue pillar at Hellenic Technologies. After every project delivery, offer clients the appropriate managed service retainer to ensure ongoing support and results. A delivered website with no maintenance retainer, or an analytics implementation with no ongoing oversight, is a missed revenue opportunity — and a risk to client success.

## Service Catalog

| Service | What's Included |
|---|---|
| **Managed Servers** | Server monitoring, software and security updates, patch management, uptime guarantee |
| **Website Maintenance** | WordPress core and plugin updates, content changes, bug fixes, security monitoring |
| **Managed BI** | Dashboard maintenance, data pipeline monitoring, monthly BI reports, ad hoc query support |
| **Managed Web Analytics** | GTM/GA4 maintenance, tag updates, monthly analytics reports, optimisation recommendations |
| **Managed Digital Marketing** | Campaign management across agreed channels, ongoing optimisation, monthly performance reporting |
| **Managed Design** | Monthly pool of design credits for ongoing creative needs — social, ads, print, web |
| **Retainer Hours** | Flexible monthly bank of hours usable across any Hellenic Technologies service |
| **SLA/BAU Retainer** | Formal SLA with defined response and resolution times for business-as-usual support |

## Main Retainer Types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Managed Service Retainers" icon="gears">
    Service-specific ongoing support — Managed Servers, Website Maintenance, Managed Marketing, Managed BI, Managed Analytics, Managed Design. Scoped and priced per service. Best for clients with a clear, ongoing need in one or more service lines.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Retainer Hours" icon="clock">
    A monthly bank of flexible hours (e.g., 10h, 20h, 40h) usable across any HT service. Ideal for clients with varied needs who want a single retainer covering multiple areas. Hours tracked monthly; report sent to client at month end.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SLA/BAU Retainer" icon="file-contract">
    A formal Service Level Agreement defining response and resolution times for ongoing BAU support. Suited to enterprise clients or those with mission-critical systems. Tiered priority levels with contractual response times.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Managed Digital Marketing" icon="bullhorn">
    Full campaign management across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, or any agreed channel mix. Includes ongoing optimisation, A/B testing, and monthly reporting. Works alongside hellenicAI ad tools for performance uplift.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Upsell Guidance

Every project delivery is an upsell opportunity. Use the table below to match the completed project to the right after-sales pitch:

| Project Delivered | Recommended After-Sales Offer |
|---|---|
| Website launch (any type) | Managed Servers + Website Maintenance |
| Analytics implementation (GTM/GA4) | Managed Web Analytics |
| BI dashboard or data pipeline | Managed BI |
| Ad campaign setup | Managed Digital Marketing |
| Branding or creative project | Managed Design |
| Multiple services / complex engagement | Retainer Hours or SLA/BAU Retainer |
| hellenicAI platform implementation | hellenicAI subscription + Managed Digital Marketing |

Make the upsell conversation part of the project handover — not an afterthought. Frame it naturally: "Now that we've built this, let's make sure it keeps performing."

<Tip>
  When pitching any managed service retainer, frame it as **protecting the client's investment** and ensuring ongoing performance — not just as a support contract. A website without maintenance degrades. Analytics without oversight drifts. Campaigns without optimisation waste budget. The retainer is the insurance policy on the work they've already paid for. This framing resonates with decision-makers far more than a feature list.
</Tip>

## Escalation & Ownership

Each managed service client should have a named **Account Manager** as the primary contact and a named **technical owner** within the relevant team. Monthly check-ins are the minimum — quarterly reviews are recommended for longer-term retainer clients.

For SLA/BAU retainers, ensure the incident management process is documented and shared with the client at onboarding. Response time SLAs must be honoured — if you're approaching an SLA breach, escalate immediately to the team lead.
