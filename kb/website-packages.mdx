## Detailed Package Breakdown

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Falcon">
    ### Falcon — Commercial Template Sites

    Falcon builds are based on high-quality commercial or premium WordPress themes and page-builder templates. The focus is on fast, cost-effective delivery without compromising on visual quality.

    **Who it's for:** SMBs, startups, and clients who need a professional web presence quickly and don't require bespoke design or complex functionality.

    **What's included:**
    - Selection of a suitable premium WordPress theme
    - Theme configuration, customisation within template constraints
    - Core pages: Home, About, Services, Contact (and up to ~5 additional pages)
    - Basic SEO setup (meta titles, descriptions, sitemap)
    - Responsive mobile layout (inherited from template)
    - Contact form and Google Maps integration

    **Limitations:**
    - Design is constrained by the template's structure and components
    - Custom design elements or layouts outside the template require a package upgrade
    - Not suitable for complex functionality or enterprise integrations

    **Technology:** WordPress + selected premium theme/page builder (Elementor or equivalent)

    **Typical timeline:** 2–4 weeks from design sign-off
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Lynx">
    ### Lynx — HT Signature Template Sites

    Lynx builds use Hellenic Technologies' own in-house signature templates. These are templates designed and maintained by our team, offering stronger brand alignment and a more distinctive look than off-the-shelf commercial themes.

    **Who it's for:** Businesses that want the quality feel of a Hellenic Technologies site and better brand differentiation, but don't need a fully custom build.

    **What's included:**
    - Access to HT's curated library of signature templates
    - Greater flexibility than Falcon — more customization within the HT template system
    - Core pages plus expanded page set as scoped
    - On-brand typography, colour palette customisation
    - SEO setup and performance optimisation
    - Responsive and mobile-optimised layout

    **Advantages over Falcon:**
    - HT-designed templates look and feel more premium and distinctive
    - Better alignment with HT's design standards — useful when clients want to reference our work in portfolio pitches
    - Slightly more flexibility in layout and component use

    **Technology:** WordPress + HT signature theme

    **Typical timeline:** 3–5 weeks from design sign-off
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mammoth">
    ### Mammoth — Fully Custom Design & Development

    Mammoth is our flagship web product: a completely custom-designed, custom-developed website built from the ground up with no template constraints. Every element is purpose-built for the client's brand, audience, and business objectives.

    **Who it's for:** Enterprises, brands with strong identity requirements, clients needing complex functionality or integrations, and any project where generic templates simply won't do.

    **What's included:**
    - Full UI/UX design process (discovery, wireframes, prototypes, final design)
    - Custom Figma design system delivered to client
    - Development built precisely to design — no template compromises
    - Custom post types, advanced functionality, third-party integrations
    - Performance, accessibility, and SEO baked in from the start
    - Enterprise-level QA process

    **Technology:** WordPress (custom theme) or React/Next.js depending on scope — agreed during discovery

    **Typical timeline:** 6–12 weeks depending on complexity and page count

    <Warning>
      Mammoth projects require a thorough discovery and scoping phase before any proposal is issued. Skipping discovery leads to scope creep and missed timelines. Never quote a fixed price for a Mammoth build without a completed scoping document.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## When to Recommend Each Package

Use this as a quick reference during sales calls and scoping conversations:

| Situation | Recommended Package |
|---|---|
| Startup or SMB, limited budget, needs site quickly | **Falcon** |
| Business wants HT quality but can't stretch to full custom | **Lynx** |
| Enterprise, complex requirements, unique brand | **Mammoth** |
| Client needs AI agents embedded in the site | **Agentic Websites** (see separate page) |
| Tight deadline (under 3 weeks) | **Falcon only** — set expectations clearly |
| Existing Figma design from client's own designer | **Figma to WordPress** add-on |

<Tip>
  Use the portfolio at [hellenictechnologies.com/portfolio](https://hellenictechnologies.com/portfolio) as a live reference during sales calls. Showing real examples helps clients self-select the right package tier and anchors expectations around quality and scope before the proposal stage.
</Tip>
