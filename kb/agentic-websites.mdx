Agentic Websites are a next-generation web product from Hellenic Technologies — websites with embedded AI agents that interact with visitors, answer questions, qualify leads, and automate workflows directly from the site. This page explains what Agentic Websites are, how they differ from standard builds, which AI capabilities are available, the technology stack, and how to scope and deliver them for clients.

## What Makes Agentic Websites Different

Traditional websites are static: they display information and wait for a visitor to take action. Agentic Websites flip that model. The AI is embedded at the core of the site — not bolted on as a widget — and actively engages visitors, responds in real time, and connects to back-end systems to take action on behalf of the business.

This is not a chatbot pop-up on top of a standard site. The AI interaction points are planned from the design phase and are built as first-class features of the website itself.

<Note>
  The Hellenic Technologies main website at [hellenictechnologies.com](https://hellenictechnologies.com) is itself built using Agentic Website technology. When pitching this product to clients, you can use our own site as a live reference.
</Note>

## Technology Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| **Front-end** | Next.js (web apps / custom builds) or WordPress (CMS-based builds) |
| **AI Layer** | hellenicAI agents — configured via [app.hellenicai.com](https://app.hellenicai.com) |
| **CRM Integration** | Connected via API to client's CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, custom, etc.) |
| **Calendar Integration** | Google Calendar, Outlook, or client's booking system |
| **Email Integration** | Automated follow-up sequences triggered by AI interactions |

The base site is built to Mammoth-level quality — there is no Agentic Website on a Falcon or Lynx base. The build quality and customisation are always at the enterprise/custom level.

## Scoping & Delivery Process

Agentic Websites require more thorough discovery than a standard build because you need to map AI interaction points before design begins. Follow this process:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery: Map AI Agent Requirements">
    Work with the client to identify which AI capabilities they need and where. Document: which pages need AI interaction points, what the AI should be able to answer or do, what systems it needs to connect to (CRM, calendar, email), and what a successful AI interaction looks like for their business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design with AI Interaction Points in Mind">
    The UI/UX Designer designs the site with AI touchpoints as intentional design elements — not afterthoughts. Conversation flows, lead capture forms triggered by AI, and interactive zones must all be wired into the Figma design before development starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the Base Website">
    Develop the site to Mammoth-level quality. Use Next.js for web-app-style builds or WordPress with a custom theme for CMS-heavy sites. The site must be on staging throughout this phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate hellenicAI Agents">
    Configure the required AI agents on the hellenicAI platform at [app.hellenicai.com](https://app.hellenicai.com). Set up the knowledge base, define conversation flows, configure lead qualification logic, and connect the agent to the website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Integrations">
    Wire up the CRM, calendar, and email integrations. Test data flows end-to-end — a lead captured by the AI should appear in the client's CRM within seconds. Do not proceed to launch without verifying all integrations are live and accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test AI Flows End-to-End">
    Run comprehensive tests of every AI flow: chat interactions, lead qualification paths, FAQ handling, edge cases, and fall-back behaviours when the AI cannot answer. Include the client in testing so they can validate that the AI responses meet their expectations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch and Monitor">
    Go live following the standard launch checklist. After launch, monitor AI performance metrics in the hellenicAI dashboard for the first 2–4 weeks. Flag any low-confidence responses or common questions the AI is failing to handle, and update the knowledge base accordingly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Timeline

Agentic Websites typically deliver in **4–8 weeks** from discovery sign-off, depending on:

- Number and complexity of AI agents required
- Number of third-party integrations
- Size and complexity of the website itself
- Speed of client feedback and content supply

Complex builds with multiple integrations and large page counts will sit at the higher end of this range. Set this expectation clearly during scoping.
