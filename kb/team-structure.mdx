Hellenic Technologies operates as a full-service digital agency with 100+ specialists organised across dedicated departments. Each department owns a distinct service area and maintains its own internal processes, but effective delivery almost always depends on cross-department collaboration. Use this page to understand who does what, which roles sit where, and how to route a task or request to the right team quickly.

## Departments at a Glance

### 🤖 AI Division — hellenicAI

The AI Division develops and manages the hellenicAI product suite, including AI Assistants, the OmniChannel Chatbot (OmniAI), B2B AI Outreach, and AI-powered advertising tools. The team handles product development, client configuration, model integrations, and ongoing optimisation of AI-driven workflows.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| AI Product Lead | Owns the hellenicAI roadmap and product strategy |
| AI Engineer | Builds and maintains AI assistant configurations and integrations |
| AI Specialist | Client-facing configuration, prompt engineering, and QA |
| Account Manager (AI) | Manages client relationships for hellenicAI accounts |

---

### 📣 Digital Marketing

The Digital Marketing department delivers paid media, organic growth, and performance marketing services across all major channels. The team manages Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, programmatic campaigns, SEO/AEO, and Social Media management.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Head of Digital Marketing | Oversees strategy, team capacity, and client performance |
| Paid Media Specialist | Manages PPC campaigns across Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn |
| SEO / AEO Specialist | Technical SEO, content strategy, and answer engine optimisation |
| Social Media Manager | Content calendar, community management, and organic social |
| Performance Analyst | Reporting, attribution, and campaign optimisation |

---

### 🌐 Web Development

The Web Development department designs and builds client websites and web applications using Hellenic Technologies' three core frameworks — Falcon (commercial templates), Lynx (signature HT templates), and Mammoth (fully custom builds) — as well as Agentic Websites, eCommerce solutions, booking systems, car rental platforms, and bespoke web apps.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Head of Web Development | Technical direction, resource planning, and delivery oversight |
| Senior Developer | Leads complex Mammoth and Agentic Website projects |
| Frontend Developer | HTML/CSS/JS, WordPress, WooCommerce, Figma-to-build |
| Backend Developer | Server-side logic, APIs, integrations, and web apps |
| QA Engineer | Testing, browser compatibility, and pre-launch checklists |
| Project Manager (Web) | Sprint planning, client liaison, and deadline management |

---

### 📊 Web Analytics

The Web Analytics team ensures accurate, compliant data collection and reporting for all client properties. Services include Google Tag Manager implementation, GA4 configuration, server-side tracking, browser tracking, and digital compliance (GDPR/cookie consent).

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Analytics Lead | Strategy, audits, and quality assurance across all implementations |
| Analytics Engineer | GTM containers, GA4 setup, server-side tracking |
| Tracking Specialist | Tag audits, event tracking, and compliance configuration |

---

### 📈 hellenicBI

The hellenicBI team provides business intelligence consulting, data engineering, and managed BI services. They build data pipelines, data warehouses, and Looker Studio / BI dashboards, and own marketing attribution modelling for enterprise clients.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| BI Consultant | Discovery, scoping, and client advisory for BI projects |
| Data Engineer | Data pipelines, BigQuery, warehousing, and ETL processes |
| BI Developer | Dashboard design and build in Looker Studio and other BI tools |
| Managed BI Analyst | Ongoing reporting and maintenance for managed BI clients |

---

### 🎨 Creative Design

The Creative Design department handles all visual output: web design, brand identity, packaging design, print materials, and Figma-to-WordPress production handoffs. They work closely with Web Development and Digital Marketing on both client-facing and internal projects.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Creative Director | Brand strategy, design quality, and team direction |
| UI/UX Designer | Web design, wireframes, and Figma prototypes |
| Graphic Designer | Branding, print, packaging, and social creatives |
| Motion Designer | Animated assets, video ads, and social content |

---

### ⚙️ DevOps

The DevOps team manages cloud infrastructure, server architecture, and CI/CD pipelines. They support internal systems and deliver managed DevOps and cloud migration services to clients, ensuring uptime, scalability, and security across all hosted properties.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| DevOps Lead | Architecture decisions, cloud strategy, and team oversight |
| Cloud Engineer | AWS / GCP / Azure provisioning, migration, and optimisation |
| Systems Administrator | Server management, monitoring, and incident response |

---

### 🤝 After Sales / Client Success

The After Sales department manages all post-delivery client relationships. This includes managed server hosting, website maintenance retainers, managed BI, managed web analytics, managed digital marketing, managed design retainers, and SLA/BAU agreements.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Client Success Manager | Retainer health, renewals, and client satisfaction |
| Support Specialist | BAU requests, ticket management, and SLA compliance |
| Maintenance Developer | Website updates, plugin management, and technical support |

---

### 💼 Account Management

Account Managers serve as the primary point of contact between clients and internal delivery teams. They manage project scopes, timelines, budgets, and client communications across all service lines.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Senior Account Manager | Manages key accounts and strategic client relationships |
| Account Manager | Day-to-day client communication and project coordination |
| Account Executive | Supports AMs on admin, reporting, and client materials |

---

### 🏢 Operations

The Operations team keeps the agency running — from HR and finance to IT access, tooling, process improvement, and internal knowledge management. If you're unsure where a request belongs, Operations is your first port of call.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Operations Manager | Process ownership, resource planning, and cross-team coordination |
| HR & People Lead | Hiring, onboarding, culture, and staff development |
| IT / Systems Admin | Tool access, licences, equipment, and security |
| Finance Lead | Invoicing, vendor payments, and financial reporting |
